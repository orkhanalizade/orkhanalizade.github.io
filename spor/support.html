Spor

Automatic mileage logbook for Sweden, Denmark, Norway and the Netherlands, with a general format for everywhere else. Made by Orkhan Alizada.

Automatic recording

Open Settings → Recording health in Spor. Background recording needs Location access (Always) and Motion & Fitness access. Your iPhone's location, Low Power Mode and background settings can affect detection. Connecting Spor to your car's Bluetooth or CarPlay makes recording more reliable. If you decline access, manual journeys still work. Check your logbook regularly and correct incomplete journeys.

Try Spor without driving

Use Settings → Recording → Add sample trip, or add a manual trip with + in the Logbook. Sample trips are labelled.

Restore purchases

If Spor Pro is locked on a new device or after reinstalling, tap Restore Purchases in Settings or on the Spor Pro screen. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

Manage or cancel a subscription

In iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Spor. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Deleting the logbook or the app does not cancel a subscription.

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Reports

The free plan exports the current month as CSV. Spor Pro adds PDF and Excel reports for any period. Review addresses, purposes, odometer readings and your country's required fields before sharing a report.

Tax rules

Spor is not affiliated with any tax authority. Rates and rules change; always check them with your tax office.

Privacy

Spor has no account, and your logbook stays on your device unless you turn on iCloud sync or share an export. Anonymous usage data can be turned off in Settings → Privacy and data. Read the privacy policy.
