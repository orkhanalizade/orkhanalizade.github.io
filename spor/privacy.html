Spor Privacy Policy

App: Spor: Mileage Log
Effective date: 15.10.2026
Publisher: Orkhan Alizada
Contact: orkhanalizada@hotmail.com

Spor is a mileage logbook. There is no Spor account and no developer-operated server that stores your journeys. Apple and RevenueCat process purchases. TelemetryDeck receives anonymous usage events, which you can turn off at any time. Spor does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Your logbook stays on your device

  • Journeys. Routes, addresses, vehicle details and plates, purposes, customers, edits and odometer readings are stored in the app's local storage.
  • Location and motion. Location and Motion & Fitness access let Spor detect and record car journeys in the background. You can log journeys manually without these permissions.
  • Odometer photos. Reading the odometer with the camera is done on your device. Spor does not send odometer photos to a text-recognition service.
  • Exports and backups. PDF, Excel, CSV and JSON files are created on your device and leave it only when you share them. Files you share are handled by the destination you choose.

Apple services

  • iCloud sync (Spor Pro, off by default). If you turn it on, your logbook syncs through your own private iCloud database. The publisher cannot access it.
  • Apple Maps. Apple may process the map area shown in the app.
  • Address lookup (off by default). If you turn on Settings → Privacy and data → Look up addresses using Apple, the start and end coordinates of a trip are sent to Apple to find addresses. Saved places are matched on your device.

These services are subject to Apple's privacy policy.

Purchases

Spor Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes payments; the publisher never receives card details. RevenueCat validates purchases and keeps your Pro status using an anonymous app-user identifier it generates, your purchase history and limited device information. Spor does not send your routes, addresses, plates or customer details to RevenueCat.

Anonymous usage data

Spor sends anonymous usage events to TelemetryDeck to understand which features are used and to improve the app. Events include general app and device information and limited details such as where a journey came from (automatic or manual), a distance range, a category, an export format and whether recording permissions are granted. Spor never sends exact coordinates, addresses, plates, customer names or driver names.

You can turn this off at any time: Settings → Privacy and data → Share anonymous usage data. When it is off, Spor sends no further usage events.

Data retention and deletion

  • On your device: Settings → Privacy and data → Delete all data removes the logbook. Deleting Spor also deletes its local data.
  • iCloud: manage or delete synced copies through your Apple Account.
  • TelemetryDeck: anonymous events are kept under TelemetryDeck's retention rules and cannot be linked back to you.
  • RevenueCat and Apple: purchase records are kept as long as needed to verify purchases and restore Pro access, under their own retention rules.

Deleting your logbook or the app does not cancel a subscription. Manage subscriptions in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Your choices

  • Stop usage data: turn off Settings → Privacy and data → Share anonymous usage data.
  • Revoke permissions: iOS Settings → Spor.
  • Questions or deletion requests: email orkhanalizada@hotmail.com. Information you email is used only to respond to your request.
