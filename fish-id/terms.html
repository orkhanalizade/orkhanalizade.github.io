6. Edibility, handling and fishing rules

Information about whether a fish is safe to eat, how to handle it, what it looks like next to similar fish, and how to catch it is general information. It is not a safety guarantee. Some fish are poisonous, venomous or unsafe to eat even when they look like a safe species. Never eat or handle a fish based only on what the App tells you. If you are not sure what a fish is, do not eat it.

Fishing rules differ by place and change over time: size and catch limits, seasons, licences, protected species and closed waters. The App does not tell you what is legal where you are. The "Fishing rules in your state" link opens a state or national agency website, which I do not control.
