Photos to PDF

Mac app that turns photos, JPG, PNG and HEIC images into one PDF, and merges them with existing PDFs. Made by Orkhan Alizada.

Restore Pro

If you bought Photos to PDF Pro and it is locked on another Mac or after reinstalling:

  1. Open Photos to PDF.
  2. Choose Help → Restore Purchase, or Settings → Restore Purchase.
  3. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

Pro is a one-time purchase. There is no subscription to cancel.
