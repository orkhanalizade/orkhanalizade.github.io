Stopwatch+

Multi stopwatch and lap timer that works like a pocket instrument: big keys, a printed lap tape, several channels at once, study blocks and a week of memory. Made by Orkhan Alizada.

Privacy Policy

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas: orkhanalizada@hotmail.com, or SET → Send feedback in the app.

Please include your iPhone model and iOS version. I usually reply within a few days.

Restore purchases

  1. Open Stopwatch+ and tap SET (top right).
  2. Tap Restore purchases.
  3. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

Manage or cancel a subscription

  • In Stopwatch+: SET → Manage subscription.
  • Or in iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Stopwatch+.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where did my older sessions go?

Nothing is deleted. The free version shows the last 7 days in MEM; older sessions stay saved on your iPhone and unlock with Pro.

The Lock Screen timer is missing

Turn on Live Activities in iOS Settings → Stopwatch+. The timer appears on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island while a stopwatch is running.

Privacy

Stopwatch+ has no account and no ads, and your sessions stay on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.
