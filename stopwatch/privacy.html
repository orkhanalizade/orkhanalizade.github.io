Stopwatch+ Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10.10.2026
App: Stopwatch+ (Multi Stopwatch & Lap Timer)
Publisher: Orkhan Alizada
Contact: orkhanalizada@hotmail.com

Data that stays on your device

Your timers, laps, sessions, tapes, notes, sets, channel names, study blocks and settings are stored only on your iPhone. They are not uploaded to the publisher. Live Activities and the Dynamic Island run on your device. Stopwatch+ has no login account.

A tape image or CSV file leaves your device only when you choose to share or export it with the iOS share sheet; you decide where it goes.

Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

Apple processes all payments; Stopwatch+ never receives your card or payment details. RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) validates App Store transactions and keeps your Pro status. RevenueCat receives purchase information (product, price, purchase and renewal dates, receipt data), basic device and app information, and an anonymous app-user identifier that it generates. Stopwatch+ does not send your name, email or session data to RevenueCat. This data is used to unlock Pro, restore purchases and show the publisher aggregated sales reports.

Analytics (Google Firebase)

Firebase Analytics receives usage events such as app opens, which screen mode is used, laps recorded, sessions saved, paywall views and purchases, together with simple counts (for example the number of laps or channels) and whether you have Pro. It also receives an app-instance identifier generated by the SDK, device and app metadata (model, iOS version, app version, language) and a general location that Google derives from your IP address. Events never include your session names, notes, lap times or channel names.

Stopwatch+ does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, so iOS does not share your advertising identifier. Stopwatch+ does not show ads, does not set an analytics user ID, does not link analytics to RevenueCat identifiers, and does not use your data for advertising or to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Feedback

If you send feedback from Settings, the topic and message you write, plus the app version, device model and iOS version, are stored in Firebase Realtime Database so the publisher can read them. Please don't include personal information you don't want to share. Feedback is not linked to your purchases or analytics.

Retention and deletion

Deleting Stopwatch+ removes everything stored on your device. Purchase records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat, and analytics data by Google, under their own retention rules. Feedback is deleted when it is no longer needed. To ask for access to or deletion of feedback or other data related to you, email orkhanalizada@hotmail.com.

Children

Stopwatch+ is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third parties

RevenueCat and Google process data on the publisher's behalf and are required to protect it at least as strictly as this policy describes.

  • RevenueCat
  • Google (Firebase)

Changes and contact

This page will be updated if the policy changes, with a new effective date. Questions or privacy requests: orkhanalizada@hotmail.com.
