Restore purchases

If you bought Caller Pro and it is locked on a new device or after reinstalling:

  1. Open Caller and tap the gear icon for Settings.
  2. Under Caller Pro, tap Restore purchases.
  3. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

Manage or cancel a subscription

  • In Caller: Settings → Caller Pro → Manage subscription.
  • Or in iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Caller Pro.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Lifetime is a one-time purchase and has nothing to cancel.

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Voice scoring does not work

  • Permissions. Voice scoring needs microphone and speech recognition access. Check iOS Settings → Caller and make sure both are on. Caller also shows an Open Settings button when access is missing.
  • On-device speech model. Caller uses Apple’s on-device speech recognition. If a model is needed, the voice control shows download progress. Keep an internet connection while the model downloads, then hold the microphone again. Availability depends on your device and iOS version.
  • Input language. Choose Settings → Voice scoring → Input language. English, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese and Japanese input are supported where on-device recognition is available. Other app languages use English numbers for now; the voice control explains when English is used. Choose the spoken call language separately in Settings → Caller voice → Call language.
  • Quiet room. Hold the mic button, speak after it lights up and release when you are done. Hands-free mode listens between throws.

Audio is processed on your device and is never uploaded.
