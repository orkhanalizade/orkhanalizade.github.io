Tagdot privacy policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026

Tagdot is operated by Orkhan Alizada. Contact: orkhanalizada@hotmail.com.

No accounts, ads or tracking

Tagdot has no account system, advertising or tracking across other companies’ apps and websites. You can use the free NFC tools without an account.

Data that stays on your iPhone

NFC record contents, scan history, notes, templates, installation-salted paired-tag identifier hashes, Screen Time selections and focus history stay on your device. Extensions share focus state through Apple’s App Group. Screen Time uses individual authorization; Screen Time selections, app tokens and data never leave the device. Device backups may include local app data.

NFC access reads and writes physical tags. Written records are readable to other NFC readers. Erasing a tag does not erase copies saved elsewhere. Exporting and sharing send only the content you choose through Apple’s share sheet. Contacts access and Apple’s contact editor are used only when you choose to save a contact. Tagdot does not request device location access; location records use coordinates you enter.

Face ID or Touch ID verifies emergency unlocks locally. Tagdot receives the authentication result, never biometric data. Optional local notifications open Tagdot; they never unlock a focus session by themselves.

Purchases

Apple processes payments. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier and purchase information to provide Pro, verify access and restore purchases. This information is not linked to a Tagdot account. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy. Manage subscriptions through Apple; deleting Tagdot does not cancel them.

Anonymous feature analytics

TelemetryDeck processes anonymous feature events and technical metadata such as app and system version, language, device category and session information. Events describe feature categories and aggregate outcomes. Tagdot never sends tag contents, NFC identifiers, Wi-Fi credentials, contacts, notes, focus mode names, Screen Time app tokens or website lists. See TelemetryDeck’s privacy policy and privacy FAQ.

Deletion and your choices

Delete library content in Tagdot to remove local records. Uninstalling removes app data according to iOS behavior; restoring a backup may restore it. Tagdot has no backend or server-held NFC or Screen Time data. RevenueCat and TelemetryDeck hold only the anonymous service records described above, under their retention policies.

Email orkhanalizada@hotmail.com to request access, correction or deletion of applicable service records. Because there is no account, we may need information that helps identify the relevant anonymous purchase record; truly anonymized analytics cannot be traced back to you. You may also contact the service providers through their privacy policies.

Children

Tagdot is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

Changes

We publish changes on this page and update the effective date.
