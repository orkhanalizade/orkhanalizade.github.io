Which tags work?

Tagdot supports compatible NDEF NFC tags, including NTAG213, NTAG215 and NTAG216 stickers. Other supported NFC families can show tag information; writable capacity and supported records depend on the tag. NFC requires a compatible iPhone.

Why can’t iPhone read MIFARE Classic data?

Core NFC does not support reading MIFARE Classic memory. Tagdot cannot bypass that iPhone limitation. Detection and available identifiers depend on the card.

How do I end a block without my tag?

Use Emergency unlock in Focus and authenticate with Face ID or Touch ID. Free users have three total emergency unlocks; Pro users have three per month. Strict mode adds a 60-second wait. Timer blocks end when their timer expires.

How do I cancel Pro?

Open iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions → Tagdot, or choose Manage subscription in Tagdot Settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Lifetime Pro is a one-time purchase.
