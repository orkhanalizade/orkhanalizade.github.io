Evenday

Get to know your PCOS, day by day.

Evenday is a PCOS and cycle tracker for iPhone. Log your period and a quick daily check-in, build a few small habits, and see a likely range for your next period that stays honest when your cycles are irregular.

Tracking is free. Premium adds your full history, a weekly report on what lines up with your symptoms, meal scans with one easy swap each, and a one-page summary to take to your doctor. The doctor summary can also be bought on its own.

Your check-ins stay on your phone. There's no account, and weight is never asked.

Evenday is not medical advice and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Always talk to your doctor about your health and treatment.
