Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Evenday (shown as Evenday: PCOS & Cycle Tracker in the App Store, the "App") is published by Orkhan Alizada. This policy explains what the App does with your information.

Your health data stays on your phone

Evenday needs no account. Everything you log (period days and flow, symptoms, sleep, energy, habits, notes, meals, your goal and your cycle details) is stored only on your iPhone. It is not sent to me, not synced to any cloud and not used for advertising. Deleting the App, or using "Delete all my data" in the App, deletes it.

The doctor summary PDF is created on your phone. It only leaves your phone if you share or save it somewhere yourself.

Meal scans

Before your first meal scan, the App asks for your OK to send meal photos to OpenAI. When you scan a meal, the App sends a smaller copy of the photo to my scan service, which runs on Cloudflare. The service passes the photo to OpenAI to name the foods and suggest one swap, sends the answer back and does not store the photo. OpenAI processes it under its API terms, which say API data is not used to train its models and may be kept for up to 30 days to detect abuse. See OpenAI's privacy policy. The App also sends the anonymous app identifier described below, so the service can apply a fair daily limit, and a Firebase App Check token from Google that proves the request comes from the real App. Firebase App Check uses Apple's App Attest and does not identify you. See Firebase privacy information.

The App asks for camera or photo library access only when you choose to scan a meal. Only the photo you pick is used.

Purchases

Apple processes payments. RevenueCat checks and restores purchases using an anonymous app identifier and your purchase history. I never receive your name, email or payment details. To understand interest in Premium, the App also tells RevenueCat how many times the offer screen was shown and which screen opened it. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Anonymous usage statistics

The App uses Mixpanel for anonymous usage statistics, processed on servers in the European Union. It sends actions such as onboarding steps, check-ins saved (as counts, like your streak), scans started and finished, offer screens shown and closed, and purchase and restore steps. Each action carries the same anonymous app identifier, the App version, whether you have Premium, days since install, and the device model and iOS version that Mixpanel adds.

Health data is never sent: no symptoms, flow, cycle dates or lengths, sleep, energy, goal, meals, photos, notes or free text. Mixpanel does not use your IP address to locate you. See Mixpanel's privacy policy.

Advertising

The App shows no ads and does not track you across other apps or websites. If you installed it from an Apple Ads ad, the App sends RevenueCat Apple's attribution token (AdServices), which identifies the campaign and keyword. It also asks Apple for the campaign, ad group and keyword numbers and adds them to the anonymous statistics, to learn which ads bring people who find the App useful. This token needs no tracking permission and does not contain your device's advertising identifier.

Reminders

Reminders are optional and scheduled on your phone: a daily check-in reminder and a note when your period may be late. Opening a reminder is counted in the anonymous statistics with a short fixed name. You can turn them off in the App or in iOS Settings. The App does not use push notifications from a server.

Networking

As with any internet connection, the services the App contacts (Apple, RevenueCat, Mixpanel, Google Firebase, Cloudflare and OpenAI) receive network information needed for the connection, such as your IP address, under their own retention rules.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.

Retention and your rights

Your logs stay on your phone until you delete them or the App. RevenueCat keeps purchase data and Mixpanel keeps the anonymous statistics under their own policies. For questions or data requests (access or deletion), email orkhanalizada@hotmail.com. Include the anonymous app identifier if you can; I'll forward requests about purchase data to RevenueCat. If you're in the EU or UK, you can also contact your data protection authority.

Changes

I may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
