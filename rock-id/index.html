Rock ID

Know what you found.

Take or pick a photo of a rock, mineral, crystal, gem or fossil and the app tells you what it likely is: its type, hardness, color and how it formed. Save your finds to your own collection.

Premium adds the rock card (an estimated value and rarity range, cleaning and care, simple field tests and lookalikes), the find log and your best finds. Safety notes and the links to official collecting rules are for everyone.

The app's name in the App Store is Rock Identifier • Stone ID. It is an independent app by Orkhan Alizada.
