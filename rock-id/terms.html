Rock ID uses artificial intelligence to help identify rocks, minerals, crystals, gems and fossils from photos. It gives educational information about what it identifies, such as how it formed, hardness, an estimated value and rarity range, cleaning and care, simple field tests and lookalikes.

6. Value, safety and collecting rules

Value and rarity ranges are rough estimates for general interest, not appraisals. Real prices depend on size, quality, origin and the buyer. Ask a qualified appraiser or gemologist before you buy, sell or insure anything.

Safety notes are general information, not a safety guarantee. Some minerals are toxic or radioactive, and a wrong identification can hide that. Do not lick, eat, grind or heat a specimen, and keep unknown specimens away from small children.

Collecting rules differ by place and change over time. The App does not tell you what is legal where you are. The "Can I collect here?" links open official websites that I do not control. Always check the rules of the land owner or the responsible authority before you collect, and get permission on private land. You are responsible for following them.
